ℹ️ About This App

👨‍💻 Developed by Ghayas Ul Hassan

Smart Business Manager is a free, offline business management tool.

📱 How Data is Stored: All your data is saved in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB/LocalStorage). This means:

  • ✅ No internet connection needed after loading
  • ✅ Data stays private on your device
  • ✅ No accounts or logins required
  • ⚠️ Data is deleted if you clear browser cache
  • ⚠️ Not shared between devices
  • 💡 Always export your data regularly for backup

💾 Storage Limit: Most browsers allow 5-50MB of storage, enough for thousands of records.

🔒 Privacy: This app doesn't send data to any server. Everything runs on your computer.

🆓 Free Forever: No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no paid features.
